# Hyperspace Coordinates: The Infinite Probability Directory

> "Think of it as the map of the infinite probability coordinate directory of the Heart of Gold."

## The Concept

Hardcard Hyperspace is not just a network of nodes; it is a **deterministic probability tree**. Every location in this multiverse has a specific "coordinate" defined by its depth and its recursive shear signature.

The further you dive (increasing depth), the more you approach the "Tail End" of probability. This is where reality becomes fragile, high-entropy, and requires immense energy (Ceramic) to sustain.

## The Coordinate System

A Hyperspace Address is defined by:
1.  **Depth ($d$)**: The integer generation count from Genesis.
2.  **Shear Signature ($S_d$)**: The cumulative gravitational weight of history.
3.  **Fuel Requirement ($C_d$)**: The Ceramic Mass needed to exist at this depth.

### The Recursive Formula ($S_n$)

The "Gravity" of the address is calculated recursively:

$$ S(n) = \sigma_{local} + 0.9 \cdot S(n-1) $$

This formula is deterministic. We can predict exactly the shear at Depth 50 without ever going there.

## The "Heart of Gold" Mode

Because the system is fractal and deterministic, we don't need to "search" for a location. We just dial the coordinate (Depth) and we know the exact structural requirements to survive there.

To exist at **Depth 50** (Event Horizon), a node requires:
- **Massive Ceramic Reserves**: To counter the 0.99 Shear.

This confirms the "Tail End" hypothesis: deeply nested realities are inherently fragile, but predictable.
