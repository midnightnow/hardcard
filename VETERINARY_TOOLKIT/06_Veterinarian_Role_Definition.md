# 📋 Role Definition: Veterinarian

**[Your Clinic Logo]**

---

## Position Overview

**Reports to:** Practice Owner/Clinical Director  
**Supervises:** Veterinary Nurses, Reception Staff (clinical matters)  
**Works with:** All team members

## Primary Purpose

To provide exceptional medical and surgical care to all patients while demonstrating clinical leadership, mentoring team members, and maintaining the highest professional standards. To make confident, independent clinical decisions while fostering a culture of continuous learning and excellence.

---

## Key Responsibilities

### 1. Clinical Excellence (40%)
- Perform thorough physical examinations and accurate diagnoses
- Develop and execute treatment plans based on evidence-based medicine
- Perform surgeries with appropriate speed and precision
- Maintain complete, accurate medical records in real-time
- Stay current with veterinary medicine through continuing education

### 2. Team Leadership (25%)
- Provide clear direction to nursing staff during procedures
- Make confident decisions without over-reliance on nurse input
- Mentor and develop junior staff through teaching moments
- Model professional behavior and standards compliance
- Address performance issues promptly and professionally

### 3. Client Communication (20%)
- Build trusting relationships through clear, empathetic communication
- Provide accurate estimates and explain treatment options thoroughly
- Handle difficult conversations with professionalism and compassion
- Ensure clients understand home care instructions
- Follow up on complex cases proactively

### 4. Business Development (10%)
- Meet or exceed individual revenue targets
- Promote preventive care and wellness programs
- Identify opportunities for practice growth
- Participate in marketing and community outreach
- Maintain high client satisfaction scores

### 5. Compliance & Safety (5%)
- Follow all practice protocols and SOPs without exception
- Maintain DEA compliance and controlled drug protocols
- Ensure radiation safety and PPE usage
- Report and address safety concerns immediately
- Participate in practice meetings and training

## Immediate Corrective Actions Required

If you are rated "Below Standard" in any area:

1. **Week 1-2:** Shadow senior veterinarian for refresher
2. **Week 3-4:** Supervised practice with feedback
3. **Week 5-6:** Independent practice with daily review
4. **Week 7-8:** Re-evaluation of performance

Failure to improve to "Meets Standard" within 8 weeks will result in formal performance management.

6. **Maintain composure:** Professional demeanor always
