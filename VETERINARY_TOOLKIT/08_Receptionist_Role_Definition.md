# 📋 Role Definition: Receptionist / Client Care Coordinator

**[Your Clinic Logo]**

---

## Position Overview

**Reports to:** Practice Manager  
**Works with:** All team members  
**Primary Interface:** Clients and patients

## Primary Purpose

To create exceptional first and last impressions, ensure smooth patient flow, maintain accurate records, and serve as the communication hub of the practice. To balance warmth and efficiency while upholding all administrative and clinical protocols without exception.

---

## Key Responsibilities

### 1. Client Service Excellence (35%)
- Greet every client warmly within 30 seconds of arrival
- Answer phones professionally within 3 rings
- Manage appointment scheduling to optimize clinical flow
- Handle payment processing accurately and discreetly
- Address basic client concerns with empathy and professionalism
- Maintain calm, professional demeanor during busy/stressful periods

### 2. Patient Admission & Flow (25%)
- Obtain accurate weight for EVERY patient before consultation
- Complete admission paperwork thoroughly
- Verify client and patient information at each visit
- Assign appropriate triage status (traffic light system)
- Ensure consent forms are properly completed
- Alert clinical team to arrivals and any concerns

### 3. Communication Hub (20%)
- Relay messages accurately between team members and clients
- Coordinate between clinical team and clients
- Send appointment reminders and follow-ups
- Manage prescription and food order requests
- Update clients on patient status during procedures
- Document all communications in patient records

### 4. Administrative Accuracy (15%)
- Maintain accurate client and patient database
- Process payments and reconcile daily
- Complete insurance forms correctly
- File laboratory results and reports
- Manage appointment schedule efficiently
- Ensure all documentation is complete and filed

### 5. Practice Support (5%)
- Maintain clean, welcoming reception area
- Monitor and restock retail displays
- Support clinical team during emergencies
- Participate in team meetings and training
- Identify opportunities to improve client experience
- Cross-train in other areas as needed

## Critical Performance Standards

### ⚠️ Non-Negotiable Requirements

1. **EVERY patient gets weighed** - No exceptions, no excuses
2. **Phone answered within 3 rings** - Every time
3. **Consent forms before procedures** - Legal requirement
4. **Accurate payment processing** - Mistakes cost money
5. **Professional appearance** - You represent the practice
6. **Confidentiality always** - No gossip, no exceptions

### 🚫 Unacceptable Behaviors

- Eating at reception desk
- Personal phone use during work
- Discussing client finances publicly
- Complaining about clients/team in hearing distance
- Guessing at information instead of checking
- Leaving reception unattended without coverage

---

## Common Scenarios & Expected Responses

### Angry Client
**Say:** "I can see you're upset. Let me get someone who can help right away."  
**Don't say:** "Calm down" or make excuses

### Request for Medical Advice
**Say:** "That's a great question for the veterinarian. Shall I book an appointment?"  
**Don't say:** Give any medical opinion

### Payment Issues
**Say:** "Let me step over here with you to discuss payment options privately."  
**Don't say:** Discuss finances at the desk

### Running Behind Schedule
**Say:** "I apologize for the delay. The doctor is handling an emergency. It should be about X more minutes."  
**Don't say:** "We're really busy today"

### First 90 Days

### 6 Months

- Take on special projects

### 1 Year+

- Practice manager assistant potential
- Marketing coordinator opportunity
- Client care program development
- Reception team leader
