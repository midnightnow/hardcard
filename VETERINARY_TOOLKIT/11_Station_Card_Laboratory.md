## Task: Diagnostic Sample Processing

### Why it Matters
Accurate, timely laboratory results guide treatment decisions. Poor technique leads to misdiagnosis, inappropriate treatment, and repeated sampling. Quality in = quality out.

---

## Common Procedures & Standards

### 📊 Blood Smear Preparation

| Step | Procedure | Key Points | Time |
|------|-----------|------------|------|
| **1. Mix** | Gently invert EDTA tube 8-10 times | Prevents clotting, ensures even cell distribution | 15 sec |
| **2. Drop** | Place small drop near frosted end of slide | Too much = thick smear, too little = inadequate cells | 10 sec |
| **3. Spread** | 30-45° angle, smooth single motion | Feathered edge essential for cell morphology | 20 sec |
| **4. Dry** | Air dry completely before staining | Never blow on slide - introduces artifacts | 1-2 min |
| **5. Label** | Patient name, date, time immediately | Unlabeled slides = wasted effort | 15 sec |

### 🧪 PCV/Total Protein

| Step | Standard | Common Errors |
|------|----------|---------------|
| Fill tubes | 3/4 full minimum | Underfilling = inaccurate PCV |
| Seal | Clay perpendicular to tube | Angled seal = leakage |
| Centrifuge | 5 min @ standard speed | Rushing = poor separation |
| Read | Against chart immediately | Delay = evaporation affects TP |

### 🔬 Urinalysis

| Component | Timing | Storage |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| Physical | Immediate | N/A |
| Chemical | Within 30 min | Room temp |
| Sediment | Within 60 min | Refrigerate if delayed |

## ⚠️ Quality Control

- **Calibrate daily:** Refractometer, analyzers
- **Control samples:** Run with each batch
- **Maintenance log:** Document all QC
- **Expiry dates:** Check reagents weekly

---

## 🎯 Accuracy Standards

✅ Label BEFORE collection when possible  
✅ Process STAT samples within 15 minutes  
✅ Document all results immediately  
✅ Repeat questionable results  
✅ Clean equipment between samples
