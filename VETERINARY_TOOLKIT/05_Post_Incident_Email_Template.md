# 📧 Post-Incident Documentation Email Templates

## Template 1: First Occurrence - Coaching Focus

**Subject:** Follow-up: [Standard/Procedure] Discussion - [Date]

Dear [Name],

I wanted to follow up on our conversation today regarding [specific standard/procedure]. 

As discussed, [describe the specific standard that wasn't met] is a required protocol that ensures [explain why it matters - patient safety/legal compliance/team efficiency].

I appreciate that you [acknowledged the issue/corrected it immediately/other positive response]. This email serves to document our discussion and ensure we're both clear on expectations moving forward.

**Action items agreed upon:**
- [Specific action 1]
- [Specific action 2]
- [Timeline if applicable]

I'm confident you'll incorporate this into your routine going forward. If you need any additional training or support to meet this standard consistently, please let me know immediately.

Thank you for your professionalism in addressing this today.

Best regards,  
[Your name]  
[Title]

cc: [HR file]

## Email Best Practices

### DO:
- Send within 24 hours of incident
- Keep tone professional and factual
- Include specific dates, times, and behaviors
- Focus on standards, not personality
- Include clear expectations going forward
- Copy HR/management as appropriate

### DON'T:
- Use emotional language
- Make personal attacks
- Discuss other employees
- Make threats you won't follow through on
- Send when angry - wait and review
- Include patient identifying information

### Filing:
- Save copy in employee file
- Track patterns across team
- Review monthly for trends
- Use in performance reviews
