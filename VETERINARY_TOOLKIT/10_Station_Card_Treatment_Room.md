## Task: Medical Procedures & Patient Monitoring

### Why it Matters
The treatment room is where critical interventions happen. Efficiency, accuracy, and constant monitoring can mean the difference between routine care and emergency response. Every second counts when a patient is under our care.

---

## Step-by-Step Workflow

| Step | Procedure | Who | Time Target | Critical Points |
|------|-----------|-----|-------------|-----------------|
| **1. Prepare** | Gather all equipment BEFORE bringing patient. Check emergency drugs accessible | Nurse | 2-3 min | Prevents delays during procedure |
| **2. Baseline** | Record TPR, assess pain score, note demeanor. Document pre-procedure status | Nurse | 1-2 min | Establishes normal for comparison |
| **3. Position** | Secure patient safely with appropriate restraint. Ensure comfort with padding | Nurse/Assistant | 1-2 min | Prevents injury to patient/staff |
| **4. Access** | Place IV catheter if required. Flush and secure properly. First attempt ideal | Nurse | 2-5 min | Multiple attempts increase stress |
| **5. Monitor** | Continuous observation during procedure. Record vitals q5-15min as appropriate | Nurse | Ongoing | Never leave patient unattended |
| **6. Document** | Record all treatments, medications, observations in real-time or immediately after | Nurse | 1-2 min | Legal requirement, continuity of care |

---

## ⚠️ Critical Standards

- **Continuous monitoring** - Eyes on patient always
- **Sterile technique** - When required, no shortcuts
- **Drug calculations** - Double-check EVERY dose
- **Documentation** - If not written, it didn't happen
- **Clean as you go** - Ready for next patient/emergency
