# 📋 Role Definition: Practice Manager

**[Your Clinic Logo]**

---

## 🎯 Mission

To ensure the smooth, efficient, and profitable operation of the clinic by leading the administrative team, managing clinic resources, and fostering a positive and professional workplace culture. To act as the key support pillar for the Clinic Director and the entire team, enabling them to focus on delivering excellent patient care.

**Reports To:** Clinic Director / Practice Owner

---

## 📝 Key Responsibilities

### 1. Human Resources
- Manage staff scheduling, payroll, recruitment, onboarding, and performance reviews
- Mediate staff conflicts and champion a healthy team culture
- Ensure fair and transparent HR practices across all team members

### 2. Financial Management
- Oversee client billing and accounts receivable
- Manage clinic budgets and control inventory and ordering
- Provide regular financial reports to the director
- Monitor profitability and identify opportunities for improvement

### 3. Operational Oversight
- Ensure the clinic complies with all health, safety, and regulatory requirements
- Manage clinic maintenance, IT systems, and relationships with suppliers and vendors
- Oversee facility management and ensure a safe, clean environment

### 4. Client Service Excellence
- Act as the final point of escalation for client complaints
- Resolve issues with professionalism and care
- Monitor client service standards and implement improvements
- Champion the client experience across all touchpoints

### 5. Strategic Support
- Assist the Clinic Director with strategic planning
- Support business development initiatives
- Implement new clinic policies and procedures
- Drive continuous improvement initiatives

## 💡 Success Tips

- **Lead by example** - Your attitude sets the tone for the entire administrative team
- **Communicate proactively** - Keep both leadership and staff informed of important developments
- **Balance compassion with accountability** - Support your team while maintaining high standards
- **Think strategically** - Look beyond daily tasks to long-term clinic success
- **Invest in relationships** - Strong vendor and team relationships make everything easier
