# 📋 Role Definition: Veterinary Nurse

**[Your Clinic Logo]**

---

## Position Overview

**Reports to:** Head Nurse/Practice Manager  
**Clinical Direction from:** Veterinarians  
**Works with:** All team members  
**May Supervise:** Student nurses, Kennel hands

## Primary Purpose

To provide exceptional nursing care to all patients, support veterinarians in clinical procedures, educate clients, and maintain the highest standards of veterinary nursing practice. To work efficiently and independently within scope of practice while knowing when to seek veterinary direction.

---

## Key Responsibilities

### 1. Patient Care & Nursing (35%)
- Monitor and record vital signs accurately and promptly
- Administer medications as prescribed
- Provide compassionate nursing care to all patients
- Perform diagnostic sample collection (blood, urine, etc.)
- Maintain detailed patient records and treatment sheets
- Recognize and report changes in patient condition immediately

### 2. Surgical & Anesthetic Support (25%)
- Prepare surgical patients efficiently (clip, scrub, position)
- Monitor anesthesia with full attention
- Maintain sterile fields and assist in surgery
- Ensure patient warming throughout procedures
- Achieve swift, safe recovery protocols
- **CRITICAL:** One nurse monitors, one nurse preps - never both watching

### 3. Clinical Procedures (20%)
- Place IV catheters successfully on first/second attempt
- Perform laboratory tests accurately (PCV/TP, blood smears, etc.)
- Take diagnostic-quality radiographs
- Conduct dental prophylaxis under supervision
- Maintain treatment areas in ready state
- Complete procedures within expected timeframes

### 4. Client Education & Communication (15%)
- Educate clients on treatment plans and home care
- Demonstrate medication administration
- Provide clear discharge instructions
- Show empathy while maintaining professionalism
- Support veterinarians during difficult conversations
- Follow up on patient progress as directed

### 5. Practice Operations (5%)
- Maintain clean, organized work areas
- Monitor and order inventory before stockouts
- Follow all safety protocols and PPE requirements
- Participate in training and meetings
- Support reception during busy periods
- Complete all documentation before shift end

## Critical Performance Standards

### ⚠️ Immediate Correction Required

1. **Patient Admission:** Weight MUST be recorded before ANY treatment
2. **Anesthetic Prep:** Begin clipping IMMEDIATELY after intubation
3. **Monitoring:** Eyes on patient, not on colleague during anesthesia
4. **Documentation:** Complete before moving to next patient
5. **Medication:** Double-check EVERY dose EVERY time
6. **Communication:** Report concerns immediately, not later

### 🚫 Unacceptable Behaviors

- Guessing medication doses or dilutions
- Leaving patients unattended during recovery  
- Skipping safety protocols "to save time"
- Giving clinical advice beyond nursing scope
- Undermining veterinary decisions to clients
- "Forgetting" to document treatments

## Skill Progression Pathway

### Entry Level (0-6 months)
- Master basic restraint and handling
- Accurate vital signs monitoring
- Basic laboratory skills
- Medication administration
- Client communication basics

### Competent (6-18 months)
- IV catheter placement proficiency
- Anesthetic monitoring confidence
- Radiography positioning
- Surgical assistance
- Client education skills

### Proficient (18+ months)
- Advanced nursing procedures
- Dental prophylaxis
- Emergency response
- Mentor new nurses
- Special interest development

### Expert (3+ years)
- Lead nurse potential
- Training coordinator
- Specialty certification
- Protocol development
- Quality improvement leader
