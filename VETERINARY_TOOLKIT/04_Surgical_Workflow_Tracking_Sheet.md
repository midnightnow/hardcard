## 🎯 Purpose
Track actual vs. target times to identify bottlenecks and improve patient safety. This is for process improvement, not punishment.

## ⏱️ Target Benchmarks
- **Induction to intubation:** ≤ 2 minutes
- **Start clipping:** Immediately after intubation
- **Clipping complete:** 5-7 minutes
- **Final prep & draping:** 3-5 minutes
- **Total time to surgery start:** ≤ 15 minutes
- **Recovery temperature:** ≥ 36.5°C

## 🚦 Workflow Checklist

### Pre-Induction Ready
- [ ] IV catheter placed and flushed
- [ ] Monitoring equipment ready
- [ ] Clippers plugged in and ready
- [ ] Warming devices available
- [ ] Surgical kit confirmed sterile
- [ ] Scrub solutions prepared

### Active Prep Protocol
**REMEMBER:** Watching anesthesia is NOT active preparation. One person monitors, one person preps.

- [ ] **0-2 min:** Induce, intubate, secure tube, start monitoring
- [ ] **2-3 min:** Apply eye lubricant, begin clipping immediately
- [ ] **3-8 min:** Complete clipping, vacuum hair, initial scrub
- [ ] **8-10 min:** Move to surgery, position, final prep
- [ ] **10-15 min:** Complete draping, surgeon ready

---

## ❌ Common Time Wasters
- Waiting to start clipping "until stable"
- Both staff watching monitors
- Looking for equipment after induction
- Incomplete pre-surgery setup
- Inefficient clipping technique
- Poor communication between team members
