## Task: Clinical Examinations & Client Communications

### Why it Matters
The consultation room is where medicine meets customer service. It's where diagnoses are made, trust is built, and treatment plans are accepted or rejected. Excellence here drives clinical success and practice growth.

## Room Preparation Checklist

| Item | Standard | Check | Reset After Each Patient |
|------|----------|-------|-------------------------|
| **Cleanliness** | Spotless, no odors | ☐ | Wipe all surfaces |
| **Temperature** | Comfortable (20-22°C) | ☐ | Adjust as needed |
| **Equipment** | Stethoscope, thermometer, otoscope, ophthalmoscope | ☐ | Clean between patients |
| **Supplies** | Gauze, syringes, sample pots, gloves | ☐ | Restock immediately |
| **Computer** | Logged in, correct patient loaded | ☐ | Save and clear screen |
| **Ambiance** | Good lighting, minimal noise | ☐ | Adjust blinds/lights |

## 🎯 Consultation Flow

| Phase | Actions | Time Guide | Key Points |
|-------|---------|------------|------------|
| **1. Welcome** | • Greet by name<br>• Confirm reason for visit<br>• Build rapport | 1-2 min | First impressions matter |
| **2. History** | • Let client talk first<br>• Ask open-ended questions<br>• Clarify timeline | 3-5 min | Listen more than speak |
| **3. Examination** | • Systematic approach<br>• Explain as you go<br>• Stay calm if pet reactive | 5-10 min | Thorough but efficient |
| **4. Discussion** | • Summarize findings<br>• Explain in lay terms<br>• Present options clearly | 5-8 min | Check understanding |
| **5. Plan** | • Agree on next steps<br>• Provide written summary<br>• Book follow-up | 2-3 min | Clear action items |

**Target: 15-30 minutes total**

## 💬 Communication Excellence

### Building Trust
- **Eye contact** with client, not just computer
- **Touch** the pet throughout (if safe)
- **Empathy** for client concerns
- **Expertise** without arrogance

### Explaining Findings
- **Simple first:** "Fluffy has an ear infection"
- **Then detail:** "The medical term is otitis externa"
- **Visual aids:** Show on model/diagram
- **Check understanding:** "Does that make sense?"

### Discussing Costs
- **Be upfront:** Present estimate early
- **Itemize:** Explain what each charge covers
- **Options:** Offer gold/silver/bronze if appropriate
- **Value:** Focus on benefits, not just price

## 🚨 Difficult Conversations

### Serious Diagnosis
1. **Warning shot:** "I have some concerning findings..."
2. **Clear delivery:** Use the word (cancer, kidney failure)
3. **Pause:** Let them absorb
4. **Support:** "I know this is difficult..."
5. **Hope:** Focus on what we CAN do

### Euthanasia Discussion
1. **Quality of life:** Use objective measures
2. **Options:** Present all choices without judgment
3. **Support:** "There's no wrong decision"
4. **Process:** Explain what to expect
5. **Aftercare:** Discuss respectfully

### Angry Client
1. **Listen:** Let them vent without interrupting
2. **Acknowledge:** "I understand you're frustrated"
3. **Apologize:** If appropriate (not admission of fault)
4. **Act:** "Here's what I can do..."
5. **Follow-up:** Ensure resolution

## 📋 Documentation Standards

**During consultation:**
- Chief complaint
- History (SOAP format)
- Physical exam findings (all systems)
- Differential diagnoses
- Diagnostic plan
- Treatment plan
- Client communication

**Must be completed before next patient**

---

## ⚠️ Red Flags

Never ignore:
- Significant weight loss
- Behavior changes
- Unexplained pain
- Persistent vomiting/diarrhea
- Breathing difficulties
- Bleeding/bruising
- Seizures
- Collapse

**When in doubt, escalate immediately**
