## 🧤 Sterile Technique Standards

### Surgical Site Preparation
1. **Clip:** 20cm margins minimum, clip away from site
2. **Vacuum:** Remove all loose fur immediately
3. **Initial scrub:** Chlorhexidine/iodine, center outward
4. **Move to OR:** Maintain patient positioning
5. **Final prep:** 3 alternating scrub/alcohol
6. **Drape:** Quarter drapes, then fenestrated

### Sterile Field Rules
✅ **Only sterile touches sterile**  
✅ **Consider below table edge contaminated**  
✅ **Gowns sterile only front chest to waist**  
✅ **If in doubt, it's contaminated**  
✅ **Back to sterile field = contaminated**

## 🌡️ Patient Monitoring Standards

| Parameter | Check Frequency | Normal Range | Action If Abnormal |
|-----------|----------------|--------------|-------------------|
| **HR** | Continuous | Dog: 60-140, Cat: 140-220 | Adjust anesthesia/notify surgeon |
| **RR** | Every 5 min | 10-20 bpm | Check depth/ventilate |
| **SpO2** | Continuous | >95% | Check probe/increase O2 |
| **ETCO2** | Continuous | 35-45 mmHg | Adjust ventilation |
| **BP** | Every 5-10 min | MAP >60 mmHg | Fluids/pressors/lighter |
| **Temp** | Every 15 min | >36.5°C | Active warming |

**Document all parameters every 5 minutes**

## 🔥 Hypothermia Prevention

**Every patient, every time:**
- Pre-warm blankets before induction
- Circulating water blanket on table
- Bubble wrap extremities
- Warm IV fluids (>30 min surgery)
- Minimize clipping area when wet
- Recovery warming until >37°C

**Goal: <1°C drop per hour**

## 🚨 Emergency Protocols

### Cardiac Arrest
1. **Alert team:** "CODE BLUE IN SURGERY"
2. **Start compressions:** 100-120/min
3. **Ventilate:** 10 breaths/min
4. **Drugs:** Atropine/Epinephrine ready
5. **Document:** Times and interventions

### Hemorrhage
1. **Direct pressure**
2. **Alert surgeon**
3. **Fluid bolus**
4. **Blood products ready**
5. **Monitor PCV/TP**

## 🧹 Turnover Standards

**Between cases (10-15 minutes):**
- Remove all disposables
- Wipe all surfaces with appropriate disinfectant
- Replace anesthetic circuit if infectious case
- Restock supplies
- Document controlled drugs
- Set up for next case

**End of day:**
- Deep clean all equipment
- Service check anesthetic machine
- Restock for emergency
- Controlled drug reconciliation
- Instrument count verification
