Role Definition: Practice Manager

Mission

To ensure the smooth, efficient, and profitable operation of the clinic by leading the administrative team, managing clinic resources, and fostering a positive workplace culture. Act as key support for the Clinic Director, enabling focus on excellent patient care.

Reports To: Clinic Director / Practice Owner

Key Responsibilities

  • Human Resources: Staff scheduling, payroll, recruitment, onboarding, performance reviews
  • Financial Management: Client billing, accounts receivable, budget management, inventory control
  • Operational Oversight: Compliance, maintenance, IT systems, supplier relationships
  • Client Service Excellence: Final escalation point, complaint resolution, service standards
  • Strategic Support: Business planning, policy implementation, process improvement

Performance Standards

Skill Area Below Standard Meets Standard Exceeds Standard
Financial Acumen Inaccurate budgets, poor inventory control, late payments Manages budgets effectively, controls inventory, ensures timely collections Identifies revenue opportunities, provides strategic financial analysis
Team Management Avoids conflicts, chaotic scheduling, low morale, high turnover Fair rosters, accurate payroll, professional HR management, positive environment Develops team members, builds engagement, trusted by all staff
Problem Solving Avoids issues, blames others, no solutions for recurring problems Troubleshoots daily operations effectively (IT, complaints, equipment) Anticipates problems, implements preventative systems, crisis leadership

Role Definition: Animal Attendant/Kennel Hand

Mission

To provide exceptional daily care for hospitalized and boarding animals, maintaining the highest standards of hygiene, comfort, and observation. Serve as the eyes and ears of the clinical team for patient monitoring.

Reports To: Head Nurse, Practice Manager

Key Responsibilities

  • Daily animal care: feeding, watering, exercise, enrichment
  • Kennel cleaning and disinfection to prevent disease transmission
  • Patient observation and accurate record keeping
  • Medication administration under supervision
  • Assisting with patient restraint and handling
  • Waste management and biosecurity protocols
  • Equipment cleaning and maintenance

Performance Standards

Skill Area Below Standard Meets Standard Exceeds Standard
Animal Care Misses feedings, poor hygiene, inadequate observation Consistent care routines, proper hygiene, accurate observations Anticipates patient needs, recognizes subtle changes, calms stressed animals
Cleaning Standards Incomplete cleaning, cross-contamination risk, odors Thorough cleaning protocols, proper disinfection, fresh environment Identifies process improvements, mentors others, exceeds hygiene standards
Record Keeping Missing entries, illegible notes, delayed reporting Accurate, timely documentation of all observations and care provided Detailed observations that assist clinical decisions, proactive communication

Workstation Reference Cards

💊

Pharmacy: Dispensing & Labelling

Step Procedure Who Time
1. Check Script Read prescription carefully. Double-check patient, drug, dose, frequency Nurse/Vet < 1 min
2. Select Drug Select correct medication, check expiry date Nurse/Vet < 1 min
3. Dispense Count prescribed quantity accurately, wear gloves Nurse/Vet 1-2 mins
4. Label Print complete label: clinic details, patient/client names, drug/strength, directions, quantity Nurse/Vet < 2 mins
Five Rights: Right Patient, Right Drug, Right Dose, Right Route, Right Time. Check every time.
🏠

Kennels: Daily Care & Cleaning

Step Procedure Who Time
1. Observe Check animal's demeanor, breathing, bedding/food/water status Kennel Hand < 1 min
2. Care Fresh food/water per chart, medications, exercise/enrichment Kennel Hand 5-10 mins
3. Clean Remove patient, dispose waste, clean and disinfect all surfaces Kennel Hand 5-10 mins
4. Record Fresh bedding, return patient, record observations and intake/output Kennel Hand < 2 mins
Record observations immediately. Small details could be vital clinical signs.
📷

Imaging: X-ray Procedures

Step Procedure Who Time
1. Prepare Check patient ID, position correctly, use restraint aids/sandbags Nurse/Vet 2-3 mins
2. Safety Clear area, wear PPE, announce "X-ray", check positioning Nurse/Vet < 1 min
3. Expose Set technique (kV, mAs), expose, check image quality Nurse/Vet < 1 min
4. Label Label image with patient details, date, view, submit to vet Nurse/Vet < 1 min
Radiation safety is everyone's responsibility. Never expose without proper positioning and PPE.
🩹

Treatment Room: Patient Procedures

Step Procedure Who Time
1. Setup Gather equipment, prepare workspace, check patient chart Nurse 1-2 mins
2. Restrain Position patient safely, use appropriate restraint for procedure Nurse 1-2 mins
3. Assist Support vet during procedure, monitor patient, hand instruments Nurse Variable
4. Clean Clean equipment, disinfect surfaces, restock supplies Nurse 2-3 mins
Patient safety first. If restraint becomes difficult, stop and reassess approach.
🩺

Consult Room: Appointment Support

Step Procedure Who Time
1. Prepare Clean room, check equipment, review patient history Nurse 1-2 mins
2. Greet Welcome client, confirm patient details, take TPR/weight Nurse 2-3 mins
3. Support Assist vet with examination, restrain patient, take notes Nurse Variable
4. Conclude Schedule follow-up, provide instructions, clean room Nurse 2-3 mins
First impressions matter. A clean, organized room builds client confidence.
