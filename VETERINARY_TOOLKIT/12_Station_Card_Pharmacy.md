## Task: Medication Dispensing & Inventory Management

### Why it Matters
Medication errors harm patients and expose the practice to legal liability. Accurate dispensing, clear labeling, and proper storage are not just good practice - they're legal requirements that protect lives.

---

## Dispensing Workflow

| Step | Procedure | Who | Verification | Common Errors |
|------|-----------|-----|--------------|---------------|
| **1. Verify** | Check prescription against patient record | Nurse/Vet | Match name, weight, species | Wrong patient |
| **2. Calculate** | Confirm dose based on current weight | Nurse/Vet | Use calculator, no mental math | Decimal errors |
| **3. Select** | Choose correct drug AND concentration | Nurse/Vet | Read label 3 times | Similar names |
| **4. Count** | Use counting tray for tablets | Nurse/Vet | Count twice | Over/under dispensing |
| **5. Label** | Complete ALL required fields | Nurse/Vet | Check against original script | Missing information |
| **6. Counsel** | Explain administration to client | Nurse/Vet | Demo if needed | Misunderstanding |

---

## ⚖️ Legal Label Requirements

**EVERY label MUST include:**
- ✅ Clinic name, address, phone
- ✅ Date dispensed
- ✅ Client name
- ✅ Patient name and species
- ✅ Drug name and strength
- ✅ Quantity dispensed
- ✅ Clear dosing instructions
- ✅ Prescribing veterinarian
- ✅ Expiry date (if applicable)
- ✅ Any cautionary statements

**Missing ANY element = illegal dispensing**

## 💊 Controlled Substances

### Double-Lock Requirements
- Schedule II: Locked cabinet within locked room
- Daily count reconciliation
- Bound register entries immediately
- Witness for wastage
- Report discrepancies immediately

### Documentation
- Date, time, patient, amount
- Running balance calculation
- Prescriber signature
- Never use pencil
- No erasures/white-out

---

## 📦 Inventory Management

| Task | Frequency | Standard |
|------|-----------|----------|
| **Stock check** | Daily | Order at minimum levels |
| **Expiry check** | Weekly | Remove 30 days before expiry |
| **Temperature log** | Daily | 15-25°C for most meds |
| **Rotation** | Each delivery | First in, first out |
| **Returns** | Monthly | Document all wastage |

---

## ⚠️ Critical Safety Points

- **Never guess** - If unsure, ask
- **Check allergies** - Every time
- **Drug interactions** - Use reference
- **Pregnant staff** - Handle with care
- **Compounding** - Follow written SOPs only
