## 🎯 Success Factors

### Leadership Actions
1. **Model the standards** - No exceptions for anyone
2. **Recognize compliance** - Publicly and frequently
3. **Address non-compliance** - Immediately and fairly
4. **Support development** - Provide resources and time
5. **Communicate constantly** - Progress, challenges, wins

### Common Challenges & Solutions

| Challenge | Solution |
|-----------|----------|
| "Too busy to follow protocols" | Time study showing protocols save time |
| "We've always done it differently" | Focus on patient outcomes, not tradition |
| "This is micromanagement" | Emphasize professional standards |
| "Some people aren't following rules" | Address individually, don't lower standards |
| "It's too much change" | Implement in phases, celebrate small wins |
