# 🔧 Pocket Script Card: Handling Pushback Professionally

**Print, laminate, and keep in your pocket for quick reference**

---

## 🎯 The CALM Method

### C - Clarify the Standard
*"The requirement is [specific standard]. This isn't optional."*

### A - Acknowledge Emotion
*"I understand you're frustrated. That's okay. The standard still applies."*

### L - Lock in Expectation  
*"This needs to happen now. How can I help you complete it?"*

### M - Move Forward
*"Let's get this done, then we can discuss how to make it easier next time."*

---

## 🗣️ Quick Response Scripts

### For: "I'm too busy"
**Say:** *"I understand you're busy. This is still required. What can we postpone to make time for this?"*

### For: "We've never had to do this before"
**Say:** *"That's changing today. This is now part of the job. Let me show you how."*

### For: "This is stupid/pointless"
**Say:** *"Your opinion is noted. The requirement stands. Please complete it now."*

### For: "I forgot"
**Say:** *"Okay. Let's fix it now. Going forward, forgetting isn't acceptable."*

### For: "[Other nurse] doesn't do it either"
**Say:** *"We're discussing your performance. Please focus on meeting your standards."*

### For: Emotional outburst/tears
**Say:** *"Take a moment if you need to. When you're ready, we still need to complete this."*

### For: "You're picking on me"
**Say:** *"This is about patient care standards, not about you personally. Everyone must meet them."*

---

## 💪 Power Phrases

- *"This is the job."*
- *"Patient safety isn't negotiable."*
- *"The standard is clear. How can I help you meet it?"*
- *"Your feelings are valid. The requirement remains."*
- *"Excellence is our minimum standard."*
- *"This protects you, the patient, and the practice."*
- *"I need this done correctly, not quickly."*

---

## 🚫 Never Say

- ~~"I know it's silly but..."~~
- ~~"When you get a chance..."~~
- ~~"If you don't mind..."~~
- ~~"Sorry to bother you..."~~
- ~~"I hate to ask but..."~~
- ~~"Maybe you could..."~~

---

## ✅ Documentation Follow-Up

After any pushback incident:

1. **Document immediately:** Date, time, standard discussed, response given
2. **Email summary:** Send professional follow-up within 24 hours
3. **Copy supervisor:** Keep management informed of patterns
4. **Stay factual:** Focus on behaviors, not personalities

---

## 🎯 Remember

- **Stay calm:** Lower your voice if they raise theirs
- **Stand firm:** Don't negotiate non-negotiables  
- **Be professional:** This is about standards, not personal
- **Follow through:** Always check that the standard was met

---

*"Clarity is kindness. Standards are not suggestions."*
