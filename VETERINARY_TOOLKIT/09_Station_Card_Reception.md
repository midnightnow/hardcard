## Task: Patient Admission & Initial Processing

### Why it Matters
First impressions set the tone for the entire visit. Accurate admission ensures patient safety through proper identification and weight recording, while efficient processing reduces wait times and clinical delays.

---

## Step-by-Step Workflow

| Step | Procedure | Who | Time Target | Critical Points |
|------|-----------|-----|-------------|-----------------|
| **1. Greet** | Welcome client within 30 seconds of arrival. Make eye contact, smile, use pet's name if known | Receptionist | < 30 sec | Sets positive tone |
| **2. Verify** | Confirm appointment details, patient ID, reason for visit. Update contact information | Receptionist | 1-2 min | Prevents mix-ups |
| **3. Triage** | Assess urgency using traffic light system (Red: Emergency, Amber: Urgent, Green: Routine) | Receptionist/Nurse | < 1 min | Ensures appropriate prioritization |
| **4. Weigh** | **MANDATORY**: Obtain accurate weight in kg. Record on travel sheet AND in system | Receptionist/Nurse | < 1 min | **Never skip - required for all medication dosing** |
| **5. Consent** | Ensure consent forms completed for procedures. Explain if needed. File immediately | Receptionist | 1-2 min | Legal requirement |
| **6. Alert** | Notify clinical team of arrival. Communicate any special concerns or requests | Receptionist | < 30 sec | Enables preparation |

---

## ⚠️ Non-Negotiables

- **EVERY patient gets weighed** - No exceptions
- **Consent before procedures** - Legal protection
- **Accurate record keeping** - Update immediately
- **Professional demeanor** - Even when stressed

---

## 📞 Common Scenarios

**Aggressive/fearful pet:** *"I'll get a nurse to help us weigh Fluffy safely"*  
**No appointment:** *"Let me check what we can do for you today"*  
**Running late:** *"The doctor is running about X minutes behind"*  
**Emergency:** Alert clinical team immediately, move to triage area
