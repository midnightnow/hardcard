# 📋 Role Definition: Animal Attendant / Kennel Hand

**[Your Clinic Logo]**

---

## 🎯 Mission

To provide compassionate, attentive care to all patients in our hospital and boarding facilities, maintaining the highest standards of cleanliness, comfort, and safety. To be the watchful eyes that notice small changes in our patients' wellbeing and the gentle hands that ensure every animal feels secure and cared for during their stay.

**Reports To:** Head Nurse / Nursing Supervisor

---

## 📝 Key Responsibilities

### 1. Patient Care & Monitoring
- Provide daily care for all hospitalized and boarding animals
- Monitor patients for changes in behavior, appetite, or condition
- Administer medications and treatments as directed by nursing staff
- Ensure all patients have fresh food, water, and comfortable bedding

### 2. Hygiene & Sanitation
- Maintain impeccable cleanliness standards in all animal areas
- Follow proper disinfection protocols to prevent disease transmission
- Manage laundry and ensure adequate supplies of clean bedding
- Properly dispose of all waste materials and biohazards

### 3. Exercise & Enrichment
- Provide appropriate exercise for patients as directed
- Offer mental stimulation and comfort to stressed or anxious animals
- Recognize and respond to individual patient needs
- Create a calm, positive environment for recovery

### 4. Communication & Documentation
- Record all observations accurately on patient charts
- Report any concerns immediately to nursing staff
- Communicate professionally with clients during visiting hours
- Participate in patient handovers between shifts

### 5. Facility Maintenance
- Keep kennels, runs, and equipment in good repair
- Report maintenance issues promptly
- Assist with inventory management of kennel supplies
- Support the broader clinic team as needed

## 💡 Success Tips

- **Every patient matters** - The scared cat in cage 3 needs as much love as the friendly puppy
- **Details save lives** - That small change you notice could be the first sign of a problem
- **Cleanliness is caring** - A spotless kennel shows respect for our patients and profession
- **You are often the unsung hero** - Your consistent, compassionate care makes recovery possible
- **Ask questions** - If you're unsure about anything, always ask for clarification
