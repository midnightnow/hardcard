## 📏 Positioning Standards

### Quality Markers for Common Views

| View | Key Points | Common Errors |
|------|------------|---------------|
| **Chest** | • Taken on inspiration<br>• Include all ribs<br>• Straight, not rotated | Expiratory shot, Cut off caudal ribs |
| **Abdomen** | • Include diaphragm to pelvis<br>• Center on umbilicus<br>• Lateral includes spine | Missing organs, Off-center |
| **Limbs** | • Include joint above and below<br>• True lateral/AP<br>• Remove collars/bandages | Oblique positioning, Motion blur |
| **Spine** | • Straight alignment<br>• Include disk spaces<br>• Foam wedges for support | Rotation, Inadequate support |

## ⚡ Radiation Safety Protocol

### ALARA Principle: As Low As Reasonably Achievable

**Distance** - Double distance = 1/4 exposure  
**Shielding** - Lead equivalent 0.5mm minimum  
**Time** - Minimize retakes through proper setup

### PPE Requirements
- ✅ Lead apron (check monthly for cracks)
- ✅ Thyroid shield (mandatory)
- ✅ Lead gloves (if in primary beam)
- ✅ Dosimeter badge (wear always)
- ✅ Lead glasses (recommended)

### Exposure Limits
- Whole body: 20 mSv/year
- Extremities: 500 mSv/year
- Pregnancy: 1 mSv/9 months
- **Report ANY suspected overexposure**

## 📱 Digital Processing

- **Never delete originals** - Legal requirement
- **Window/level only** - Don't over-process
- **Label correctly** - R/L markers, date, time
- **DICOM standards** - Maintain compatibility
- **Backup daily** - Protect against loss
