## 🟣 PHARMACY STATION
**Task: Dispensing & Labelling Medications**

### Why it Matters
Accuracy in dispensing prevents medication errors that can harm patients. Clear labelling is a legal requirement and ensures client compliance and safety.

| Step | Procedure | Who | Time |
|------|-----------|-----|------|
| **1. Check Script** | Read the veterinarian's prescription carefully. Double-check the patient name, drug, dose, and frequency. | Nurse / Vet | < 1 min |
| **2. Select Drug** | Select the correct medication from the shelf. Check the expiry date. | Nurse / Vet | < 1 min |
| **3. Count & Dispense** | Count the prescribed quantity accurately using a pill counter. Wear gloves as appropriate. | Nurse / Vet | 1-2 mins |
| **4. Label Clearly** | Print a label with all required information: Clinic details, patient name, client name, date, drug name & strength, clear directions, quantity, and cautionary statements. This is a legal document. | Nurse / Vet | < 2 mins |

**🔑 Key Reminder:** The "five rights" are non-negotiable: Right Patient, Right Drug, Right Dose, Right Route, Right Time. Check every time.

## 🔵 IMAGING / RADIOLOGY STATION
**Task: Diagnostic Imaging Procedures**

### Why it Matters
Quality images lead to accurate diagnoses. Radiation safety protects both our team and our patients. Poor positioning wastes time, money, and exposes everyone to unnecessary radiation.

| Step | Procedure | Who | Time |
|------|-----------|-----|------|
| **1. Safety First** | Don appropriate PPE (lead gown, thyroid shield, gloves). Ensure dosimeter badge is worn. Check pregnancy status of all staff present. | All Staff | < 1 min |
| **2. Patient Prep** | Sedate/position patient as directed. Use sandbags and foam wedges for stability. Measure the thickest part for exposure settings. | Nurse / Vet | 2-5 mins |
| **3. Technique Chart** | Refer to technique chart for correct kVp and mAs based on body part and thickness. When in doubt, ask – don't guess. | Nurse / Vet | < 1 min |
| **4. Process & Review** | Take exposure using proper technique. Process image and check quality before moving patient. Repeat if necessary for diagnostic quality. | Nurse / Vet | 1-2 mins |

**🔑 Key Reminder:** ALARA principle - As Low As Reasonably Achievable. Minimize radiation exposure through distance, shielding, and time.

## 🟤 KENNEL & BOARDING STATION
**Task: Daily Cleaning & Patient Care**

### Why it Matters
Impeccable hygiene prevents disease transmission and ensures patient comfort. Close observation helps us identify patient needs and potential problems early.

| Step | Procedure | Who | Time |
|------|-----------|-----|------|
| **1. Observe First** | Before opening a cage, observe the animal's demeanour, breathing, and the state of their bedding/food/water. | Kennel Hand / Nurse | < 1 min |
| **2. Patient Care** | Provide fresh food and water according to the patient's chart. Administer any scheduled medications. Provide exercise/enrichment as directed. | Kennel Hand / Nurse | 5-10 mins per patient |
| **3. Clean & Disinfect** | Remove the patient to a secure holding area. Dispose of all bedding and waste. Thoroughly clean and disinfect all surfaces with appropriate chemicals. | Kennel Hand | 5-10 mins per cage |
| **4. Reset & Record** | Place fresh, clean bedding. Return the patient. Record all observations, food/water intake, and elimination on the hospital chart. | Kennel Hand / Nurse | < 2 mins |

**🔑 Key Reminder:** Small details matter. That decreased appetite or unusual behavior could be the first sign of a complication.

## 🟡 TREATMENT ROOM STATION
**Task: Medical Procedures & Patient Monitoring**

### Why it Matters
The treatment room is where critical care happens. Efficiency, sterility, and accurate monitoring can mean the difference between a routine procedure and an emergency.

| Step | Procedure | Who | Time |
|------|-----------|-----|------|
| **1. Setup & Prep** | Gather all required equipment and medications. Check emergency drugs are accessible. Ensure monitoring equipment is functional. | Nurse | 2-3 mins |
| **2. Patient Assessment** | Take and record baseline vitals (TPR). Note any abnormalities. Secure IV access if required. Apply monitoring equipment. | Nurse | 2-3 mins |
| **3. Procedure Support** | Assist veterinarian as directed. Monitor patient continuously. Anticipate needs. Maintain sterile field if applicable. | Nurse | Variable |
| **4. Recovery & Cleanup** | Continue monitoring until stable. Clean and disinfect all surfaces and equipment. Restock supplies. Document all treatments given. | Nurse | 5-10 mins |

**🔑 Key Reminder:** The patient cannot tell us when something's wrong. Your observation skills are their voice.

## 🟢 CONSULTATION ROOM STATION

## 🔴 SURGICAL SUITE STATION
**Task: Aseptic Technique & Surgical Support**

### Why it Matters
Surgical success depends on teamwork, preparation, and unwavering attention to sterile technique. Every break in protocol increases infection risk.

| Step | Procedure | Who | Time |
|------|-----------|-----|------|
| **1. Pre-Surgery Check** | Confirm correct patient, procedure, and surgical site. Check consent form signed. Fast status confirmed. Pre-medication given. | Nurse | 2-3 mins |
| **2. Prep & Position** | Clip and initial prep in prep area. Final surgical prep in theatre. Position patient appropriately with padding to protect pressure points. | Nurse | 5-10 mins |
| **3. Sterile Field** | Open surgical packs using aseptic technique. Assist surgeon with gowning and gloving. Maintain sterile field throughout procedure. | Nurse | Variable |
| **4. Recovery Protocol** | Monitor anesthesia recovery closely. Maintain airway until swallowing reflex returns. Keep patient warm. Document all medications and events. | Nurse | 15-30 mins |

**🔑 Key Reminder:** Infection control is everyone's responsibility. If you see a break in sterile technique, speak up immediately.
