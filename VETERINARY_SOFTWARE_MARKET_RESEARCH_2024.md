The veterinary software market presents a significant opportunity, valued at $1.44 billion in 2024 and projected to reach $3.01 billion by 2030 (CAGR 13.21%). ## Regulatory Requirements

### DEA Compliance (Critical for VetSorcery)
- **Registration**: DEA registration required for controlled substances
- **Record-keeping**: 2-5 year retention requirements
- **Storage**: Securely locked cabinet requirements
- **Reporting**: One business day theft/loss reporting
- **Software needs**:
  - Automated record-keeping
  - Schedule II vs III-V separation
  - User authentication and audit trails
  - Integration with PIMS
  - Error checking and auto-reconciliation

### 2024 Regulatory Updates
- Veterinary Medicine Mobility Act conforming amendments (Feb 2024)
- Allows transport/dispensing at alternate sites
- New state telemedicine regulations (AZ, CA)

### Compliance Statistics
- 37% of clinics have controlled substance discrepancies
- 27% increase in DEA registration surrenders (2014-2019)
